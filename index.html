Tumble Tower

Jenga-Style 2–4 Player Game • Custom Stack, Queue & LinkedList Project

Welcome to Tumble Tower!
How to Play Tumble Tower:
  • Turn Selection: The player at the front of the queue selects any block on or below the level under the top level.
  • Moving: Click "Remove and place on top". The block is extracted and placed in the top level (fill order: Middle → Left → Right).
  • Stability Physics: The tower collapses if a level below the top has only a single block placed on an edge (left or right slot) with the middle empty, or if fewer than 2 levels remain!
  • Undo: You can undo moves at any time before collapse. Turn queue, stacks, and counts reverse cleanly.
